Prison of Eternal Sky

I Am Both a God and a Slave

Stranded. Enslaved. Lost.

Mathew Dredon is stuck, in more ways than one, with no way home.

The land around him is dangerous and strange, filled with divine artifacts and monsters born from darkness.

Forced into a body that’s not his own by a mysterious beings with godlike powers, Mathew is left to search for answers.

He struggles relentlessly against the chains that bind him, any hope of returning home dwindling.

Only by unlocking the secrets of this new land does he stand a chance of survival.

Perhaps swallowing an unknowable cosmic entity was a questionable decision...

About

Riker Rhodes is a writer focused on dark fantasy and cosmic horror.

His work leans into themes of identity, psychology, and morality. He is particularly drawn to stories with many hidden layers of secrets and information, and where characters have to piece together truths about their reality on their own.

He takes inspiration from a range of sources, such as cosmic horror and wierd fiction, eastern storytelling from anime and webnovels, even high fantasy and classic literary works. You may find traces of Lovecraft, Edgar Allen Poe, and Robert W Chambers woven throughout his work, as well as a touch of contemporary eastern fiction.

His two main pulls to writing are that of massive worlds filled with unknowble forces and deep lore, and explorations of a characters psyche and why they behave the way they do. He aims to create works that attempt to weave the two into a coherent narrative.

If that kind of storytelling intrigues you, than we invite you to join us on our journey of discovery into the darker side of fiction.
