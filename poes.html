Overview

Welcome to my first webnovel, Prison of Eternal Sky! Here are some things about the setting and story, but some go into soft-spoiler territory so I don’t mind if you skip it.

PoES is a Portal Fantasy/Isekai/Transmigration Novel, that isn’t exactly classic fantasy (no elves or dwarfs, but maybe a dragon or two) It will dip into cosmic horror and grimdark a bit here and there, but won’t be as dark as my other writings.

There will be some direct references to the works of Lovecraft and Shakespeare here and there.

In this setting, Gods are real and scheming, and the magic system and everything about it directly relates to each God. This hopefully will make it intuitive and easy to understand.

This is NOT a progression fantasy story. The way the magic system works, there are not really clear ways to get stronger. Because of that progression will be non-linear, and the story will be more about discovering the lore and learning more about the world, and character development (And hopefully some cool character arcs)
